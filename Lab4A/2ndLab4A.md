# Chip's Chip off the old Chip-Chip Recipe

Welcome to Chip's Recipes (totally original - Jake)
The following will be the *Ingredients* and *steps* necessary to make home made Potato chips

## Ingredients:
- *2 Pounds of Russet Potatoes*
- *2 tablespoons of Salt*
- *3 Cups Canola Oil (Veg Oil will work too)*

## Step by Step Recipe:
1. Wash, skin and cut the Russets **Very thin** (aiming at an Eighth of a Inch)
2. Soak in clean water for 20 Minutes. then dry them out as it is very important to **Not** put water or wet items into boiling oil, for safety
3. Heat your oil in a pot or pan to 325*F
4. Depending on the number of Potatos you use. you may be able to lay them out interspaced across the pan. or will need to cook in batches, either way cook for **5** or more minutes, aiming for a golden brown, and do not **Crowd out** the pan!
5. Drain the oil from the Chips either on a rack or by dabbing them out with paper towels. apply your salt to taste before they fully dry or cool down. once they are drained of excess oil, you can enjoy!
